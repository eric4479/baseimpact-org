# AI Hardware & Technology Grant Research for Base Impact Inc.

## Summary

Base Impact Inc. is a nonprofit in Brevard County, FL focused on digital inclusion, technology access, and community support. This document outlines research findings on AI hardware grants, technology donations, and community AI projects that could benefit from new technology.

---

## 1. FREE AI CREDITS & SOFTWARE (No cost, apply now)

### Google for Nonprofits
- **What:** $10,000/month in Google Ad Grants, free Google Workspace, Google Cloud credits
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor (LifePointe could sponsor now)
- **Apply:** google.com/nonprofits
- **Value:** $10,000/month ad spend + free productivity tools + cloud credits
- **Note:** Ad Grants are for search advertising only, not AI compute

### Microsoft for Nonprofits
- **What:** $2,000/year Azure credits, discounted Microsoft 365, donated software
- **Eligibility:** Registered nonprofit
- **Apply:** microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits
- **Value:** $2,000/year cloud credits + discounted productivity tools
- **Note:** Recently reduced from $4,000/year to $2,000/year

### TechSoup
- **What:** Discounted hardware, software, and services from 100+ tech companies
- **Eligibility:** Registered nonprofit or library
- **Apply:** techsoup.org
- **Value:** 50-90% off retail prices on computers, monitors, networking, software
- **Note:** Best single resource for affordable hardware

### Anthropic (Claude)
- **What:** Discounted plans for verified nonprofits, social impact track
- **Apply:** anthropic.com (nonprofit program)
- **Value:** Discounted API access + social impact research track
- **Note:** Separate from startup credits — nonprofits have their own program

### OpenAI
- **What:** Discounted seats on team plans for verified nonprofits
- **Apply:** openai.com (nonprofit program)
- **Value:** Per-seat discount on ChatGPT Team
- **Note:** Not API credits — seat-based discount

### AWS Nonprofit Credit Program
- **What:** Cloud credit allocations + competitive grants pairing cash with credits
- **Apply:** aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits
- **Value:** Small credit grants up to five/six figure awards
- **Note:** Competitive grants pair cash + credits

### Canva for Nonprofits
- **What:** Free Canva Pro for nonprofits
- **Apply:** canva.com (nonprofit program)
- **Value:** Free design tools for marketing, social media, presentations

### Notion for Nonprofits

### Slack for Nonprofits

### HubSpot for Nonprofits

## 4. COMMUNITY AI PROJECTS (Ideas for Base Impact)

### Project 1: AI Form Filling Assistant
- **What:** Use local AI (Llama, Mistral) to help people fill out government forms, job applications, and housing applications
- **Hardware needed:** Laptop with 16GB+ RAM, or desktop with GPU
- **Impact:** Helps homeless and low-income individuals navigate bureaucracy
- **Grant fit:** Digital equity, digital literacy, workforce development

### Project 2: AI Document Reader for Seniors
- **What:** AI-powered tool that reads and summarizes documents, letters, and forms for elderly residents
- **Hardware needed:** Tablet or laptop with AI capability
- **Impact:** Helps seniors understand medical bills, legal documents, and government notices
- **Grant fit:** Senior services, digital literacy, health equity

### Project 3: AI Resource Finder
- **What:** Chatbot that helps people find local resources (food pantries, shelters, job help, healthcare) based on their needs
- **Hardware needed:** Server or cloud instance
- **Impact:** Connects people to resources faster and more efficiently
- **Grant fit:** Digital inclusion, community resource access

### Project 4: Digital Skills Training Lab
- **What:** Hands-on computer lab with refurbished laptops for digital skills training
- **Hardware needed:** 10-20 refurbished laptops, projector, networking
- **Impact:** Trains seniors, homeless, and low-income individuals in basic computer skills
- **Grant fit:** Digital literacy, workforce development, senior services

### Project 5: Device Refurbishment Program
- **What:** Collect donated devices, refurbish them, and distribute to people in need
- **Hardware needed:** Workbench, tools, parts, storage
- **Impact:** Extends device life, reduces e-waste, provides technology access
- **Grant fit:** Environmental, digital equity, circular economy

### Project 6: AI-Powered Translation Service
- **What:** Use local AI to provide real-time translation for non-English speakers at community events
- **Hardware needed:** Laptop with AI capability
- **Impact:** Breaks language barriers at resource fairs and community events
- **Grant fit:** Digital inclusion, language access, community engagement

### Project 7: AI Job Search Assistant
- **What:** AI tool that helps people write resumes, practice interviews, and find jobs
- **Hardware needed:** Laptop or tablet with AI capability
- **Impact:** Helps unemployed and underemployed individuals find work
- **Grant fit:** Workforce development, digital literacy, economic mobility
