# Base Impact Inc. — Impact Summary

## Who We Are

Base Impact Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (application pending) serving Brevard County, Florida. Our mission is to help people access technology, digital skills, and community resources — bridging the digital divide for homeless, low-income, senior, and underserved residents.

**Board:** 3 unrelated directors (President, Secretary, Treasurer)
**Service Area:** Brevard County, FL

## What We've Done

- Curated and published a directory of **80+ local resources** across **12 categories** in Brevard County (food, shelter, health, housing, recovery, and more)
- Provided **free technology support** to **6 local churches and nonprofits** (audio/video setup, livestreaming, Wi-Fi, computers, websites, sign-in systems)
- Volunteered **hundreds of hours** in food service, clothing distribution, cold-night shelter security, senior assistance, and housing connections
- Connected people experiencing homelessness with housing resources
- Acquired and distributed cold weather supplies to low-income elderly residents
- Trained on existing sign-in and tracking systems at local food pantries
- Became a member of the **Brevard Homeless Coalition** (Continuum of Care for Brevard County)

## Community Involvement

Base Impact's founder is deeply rooted in the Brevard County community through years of hands-on volunteer work:

- **Food service** — Serving meals at LifePointe Ministries' CREW 29 outreach (Thursday dinners) and First United Methodist Church's Community Breakfast
- **Homeless support** — Providing showers, clothing, essentials, and toiletries to people experiencing homelessness at LifePointe Ministries
- **Cold-night shelter** — Security and support at LifePointe's inclement-weather shelter (only one between Titusville and Port Orange)
- **Senior assistance** — Transportation, technology, and daily needs for elderly community members
- **Housing connections** — Connecting people living in their cars with housing resources
- **Emergency response** — Acquiring and distributing cold weather clothing to financially strained and low-income elderly
- **Technology support** — Free tech help, device setup, and digital skills training for neighbors and small nonprofits
- **Communications & safety** — Serving on safety and communications teams at local organizations and events
- **Community cleanup** — Neighborhood cleanup and beautification

## Programs

1. **Technology Access** — Helping neighbors get working devices and internet service
2. **Digital Skills & Job Help** — Walk-throughs for housing portals, job applications, benefits, staying safe online
3. **Tech for Small Nonprofits** — Free setup, security, and support for community groups
4. **Local Resource Guide** — Online directory of 80+ Brevard County services (bilingual: English/Spanish)
5. **Laptop & Device Training** — Hands-on digital skills sessions with loaned/donated devices
6. **AI & Technology Infrastructure** — Local AI tools for form filling, document reading, resource finding
7. **Cell Phone & Tablet Partnerships** — Free/low-cost phones and tablets with data plans for homeless and people in need
