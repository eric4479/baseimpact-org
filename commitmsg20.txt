Add a /socials page, and fix the footer links that ran together

New /socials page, reachable from a "Follow us" link in the footer. It lists the accounts
Base Impact actually owns, states the handle standard, and warns that other organisations
use the name.

What is on it now is one account, because one account exists. x.com/baseimpactorg is ours.
The other platforms are listed as plain text with no links, under "Not set up yet" -- a
profile URL for an account that has not been claimed would either 404 or land on a
stranger, which is exactly the failure mode this site is built to avoid.

Handle standard is baseimpactorg, and the shorter baseimpact is NOT available:

  x.com/baseimpactorg        ours
  x.com/baseimpact           TAKEN by ImpactBase, a pre-seed incubator
  instagram.com/baseimpact   TAKEN by baseimpact, UK sports science, 6 followers
  instagram.com/baseimpactorg  free -- "Profile isn't available"
  tiktok, youtube, linkedin, github, bluesky  free on both handles
  facebook, threads           could not be determined from outside (login wall)

Facebook and Threads could not be checked past their login walls. They are left out of the
page entirely rather than guessed at.

The account list lives in src/lib/socials.ts, one line per account, and feeds the page, the
footer link, and the Organization schema's sameAs. Claiming a platform is a one-line change
there; a comment in that file states the rule that only accounts we control belong in it.

ALSO FIXED, a pre-existing bug this work surfaced. The four footer links were inline-block
with mt-1, and margin-top cannot break an inline flow, so they rendered as one run-on line:
"Privacy policyTerms & disclaimerFollow usShare feedback". They are block now and each sits
on its own line, 24px apart -- which also clears WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8, since adjacent targets
with no gap and no spacing fail it. This was already broken with three links before "Follow
us" was added; the fourth just made it longer.

Audit: 0 axe violations across 56 route/viewport combinations, 0 horizontal overflow, 0
blank pages. Build clean, validator 80/80, 14 prerendered routes.
