Fix responsive layout, landmark and contrast issues found in the audit

Audited all 13 prerendered routes at 320/375/430/768/1024/1440/1920px with axe-core
(WCAG 2.1/2.2 A/AA) plus direct layout measurement.

Legal links were unreachable on every screen under 1024px. The footer carried
"hidden ... lg:block" and Privacy policy / Terms & disclaimer existed nowhere else --
the mobile tab bar is Home/Help/Partners/About/Guide/Give/Volunteer. The Terms page is
where the directory disclaimer and the liability language live, so on phones -- most of
the traffic -- neither notice was presented at all. The footer now renders at every
width and stacks into a column below sm.

The header could not fit at the breakpoint it used. Logo (116) + nav (766) + button (148)
+ gaps needs 1054px but only 992px is available at 1024px, and the "Need help now" button
is shrink-0, so it overflowed its parent by 62px and the document by 46px across the whole
1024-1149px band. The primary nav and the bottom tab bar now switch at xl (1280px), where
the row genuinely fits.

Moving the footer into view then exposed a second bug: the fixed bottom tab bar sat on top
of the legal links. pb-nav reserved its clearance on <main>, which is no longer the last
element -- tapping where Privacy/Terms render hit the tab bar instead. The clearance moved
to <footer>, and both links are now clear of the nav and tappable at 320/375/390/430/768/
1024/1280/1440.

Also from the audit:
- the inline "let us know" link in the directory was distinguished by colour alone
  (#60a5fa on #e2e8f0 body text is 2.06:1, under the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.1 requires); it is now
  always underlined rather than only on hover
- the pre-filing banner sat outside every landmark; it is now an <aside> with a label,
  which clears the axe "region" rule that fired on all 13 routes
- directory cards are h3 with nothing between them and the h1; added a visually hidden h2
  for the results region so the document outline is not skipped
- tag and service chips were exactly 24px tall, WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8's floor with no margin;
  padding raised to px-2.5 py-1.5

Result: 0 horizontal overflow across all 52 route/viewport combinations, 0 axe violations
on a direct re-run, 0 controls missing a focus indicator. The batch harness intermittently
reports 1-3 target-size findings that do not reproduce on re-run (one is a tel:911 link
styled min-h-16, i.e. 64px tall); treated as measurement flake, not a defect.

Build clean, validator 80/80, gate suite 7/7.
