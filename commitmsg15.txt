Adjust partner cards and remove CPR/first-aid listings

Three content changes to what Base Impact offers partners.

Pantries: the card asked them to "flag when food arrives". That is not information we
publish or need -- what a person looking for help actually needs is whether a pantry is
open right now, so the card now asks only for hours and an honest public schedule.

Removed CPR & first-aid trainers as a partner category. It was a card on /partners, an
option in the registration form's "What you offer" list, a mention in the /join partner
blurb, a mention in the /about partners sentence, and "first-aid training" in the home
page referrals card. All five are gone; no trace of CPR or first aid remains in the
built output. The partner grid is now two cards, so it drops from three columns to two
rather than leaving a gap.

Tiny teams: swapped bookkeeping for websites, matching the tech work we actually do
(grant paperwork, email security, getting a simple site online).

Build clean, validator 80/80, gate suite 7/7.
