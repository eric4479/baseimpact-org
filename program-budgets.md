# Base Impact Inc. — Program Budget Templates

## How to Use These Templates

Each program has a simple budget template. Fill in the numbers based on your actual needs and available funding. These are designed for grant applications — adjust as needed.

**General rule for nonprofits:** Most grants allow 10-15% for indirect/administrative costs. Direct costs (equipment, supplies, training materials) should be the majority of the budget.
