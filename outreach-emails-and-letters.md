# Email & Letter Drafts for Base Impact Inc.
# Organizations: LifePointe, No One Hungry, Hope for North Brevard, The Rock Church, Under The Bridge, First Christian Church, First United Methodist Church

## HOW TO USE THESE DRAFTS

Each organization gets one email with THREE options they can choose from:

1. **Letter of Support** — Simple letter saying they know you and support your mission
2. **Fiscal Sponsorship Agreement** — Formal agreement allowing Base Impact to receive tax-deductible donations under their 501(c)(3) status while Base Impact's own application is pending
3. **Community Partnership Agreement** — Formal partnership for joint programs, events, and projects

Send the email with all three options. The organization picks which one(s) they want to do. You can also combine them (e.g., letter of support + community partnership).

---

## EMAIL TEMPLATE (send to each organization)

Subject: Base Impact Inc. — Community Partnership & Support Opportunities

Dear [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to share an update about Base Impact Inc., a nonprofit I founded to help people in Brevard County access technology, digital skills, and community resources.

As you may know, I've been volunteering with [Organization Name] for some time now — helping with [specific activities]. I'm reaching out because Base Impact is currently preparing its 501(c)(3) application and I'd like to formalize our relationship.

I'm offering three options, and you can choose any combination that works for your organization:

**Option 1: Letter of Support**
A simple one-page letter stating that you know me and support Base Impact's mission. This helps with grant applications and our 501(c)(3) filing. No commitment or obligation beyond the letter itself.

**Option 2: Fiscal Sponsorship**
A formal agreement allowing Base Impact to receive tax-deductible donations under your 501(c)(3) status while our own application is being processed. Base Impact would handle all program expenses and reporting. This is temporary until we receive our own determination letter.

**Option 3: Community Partnership**
A formal partnership agreement for joint programs, events, and projects. This could include co-hosting digital skills workshops, device distribution events, or community resource fairs. Terms are flexible and can be defined together.

I've attached a draft for each option. Please review them and let me know which one(s) work best for your organization. There's no pressure — I value our relationship regardless of the formal path we choose.

If you have any questions, I'm happy to meet or discuss by phone.

Thank you for everything you do for our community.

Warm regards,
Eric

## OPTION 2: FISCAL SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT (outline)

FISCAL SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

Between: [Organization Name] ("Sponsor") — EIN [EIN]
And: Base Impact Inc. ("Project") — EIN [Base Impact EIN]

Effective Date: [Date]

1. PURPOSE
Sponsor agrees to serve as fiscal sponsor for Project, allowing Project to receive tax-deductible donations under Sponsor's 501(c)(3) status while Project's own 501(c)(3) application is pending.

2. TERMS
- Sponsor will receive and manage donations designated for Project
- Sponsor will disburse funds to Project upon request for approved program expenses
- Project will provide quarterly financial reports to Sponsor
- Project will maintain its own bank account and financial records
- This agreement is temporary and will terminate upon Project receiving its own 501(c)(3) determination letter
- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice

3. RESPONSIBILITIES
- Sponsor: Receive donations, issue tax receipts, disburse funds, file required reports
- Project: Use funds for approved programs, provide reports, maintain records

4. FEES
- Sponsor may retain [X]% of donations for administrative costs (or $0 if waived)

5. TERMINATION
- Upon Project receiving 501(c)(3) status, or by mutual agreement
- Project will transition to independent status upon termination
