Move Feedback to the footer, and make the pantry card warn about availability

Feedback was the eighth item in the desktop nav while the mobile tab bar carried only the
seven main destinations, so the two navigations disagreed. It now appears only in the
footer, where "Share feedback" already sat alongside Privacy policy and Terms -- the nav is
the seven destinations on both, and the duplicate is gone. Removed the now-unused
MessageSquare import.

Pantries & shelters: the card asked partners to "keep the public schedule honest", which
does not address what actually goes wrong. Pantries and shelters do not update us often,
so a posted schedule can be stale and someone can travel there and find the doors shut.
The card now asks partners to tell people to call first and says so explicitly: "Post your
hours and ask people to call first. Availability changes -- a phone call beats a wasted
trip." This matches the "Call to Confirm" tag already used across the directory listings.

Also re-tested the header breakpoint. Removing Feedback cut the nav from 766px to 651px,
which on paper brings the row inside 1024px -- and my earlier note said that was the
alternative to the xl switch, so it was worth measuring rather than assuming. It does not
hold up: at 1024-1032px the row fits with exactly 0px of slack, and the shrink-0 "Need help
now" button measures 148px inside a 135px wrapper, overflowing its parent by 13px. That is
the same failure as the original 62px overflow, just below the threshold where it becomes
visible page overflow. Kept the nav at xl, where there is ~97px of slack.
